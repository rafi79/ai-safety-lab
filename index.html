Inside the step

What is simplified here?

The model has fixed, untrained deterministic weights, a tiny vocabulary and 8-dimensional vectors. Text uses a teaching tokenizer, not BPE. Images use 16 RGB-mean patches; audio uses 8 waveform feature windows, not a trained encoder. Position uses sinusoidal encoding. Values and causal attention are computed, but they are not activations from ChatGPT or a pretrained model. Probabilities are not meaningful language predictions.
